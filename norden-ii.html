Norden II

Build the loadout before the launch.

Norden II is the strike-planning and loadout intelligence engine inside the Vanguard C4 ecosystem.

The module helps commanders and pilots move from aircraft selection to weapon station detail, mission fit, validation state, and a package that can be reviewed by S.C.O.U.T. in operational context.

Primary Proof

The loadout manager is the tactical center of gravity.

This is the strongest proof point because it immediately communicates aircraft, hardpoints, mission profile, ordnance, package awareness, and combat planning in one operational view.

Connected to S.C.O.U.T.

The commander should see the loadout in mission context.

Norden II builds the tactical package. S.C.O.U.T. reviews the package against mission number, force allocation, readiness, and commander intent. That separation keeps each system focused.

Operational principle: Norden II builds and validates the loadout; S.C.O.U.T. approves it in the command workflow.
