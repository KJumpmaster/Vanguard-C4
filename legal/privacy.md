## Overview

Static Line LLC ("we," "us," or "our") develops and distributes software tools including **SCOUT Tactical Mission Management**, **NORDEN II Tactical Strike Planning Suite**, and **Key Forge**. This Privacy Policy explains what information our applications collect, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Our applications are designed to store your data locally on your own device or your own server infrastructure. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 User-Generated Data

Our applications allow you to create and store data including:

- Mission names, dates, objectives, and operational details
- Personnel callsigns, roles, and participation records
- Loadout configurations, weapon data, and planning parameters
- After-action review notes, lessons learned, and comments
- Any other content you choose to enter into the application

**This data is created by you and stored locally on your device or your own server.** Static Line LLC does not have access to this data and does not receive copies of it.

### 1.2 Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

- Names, email addresses, or contact information
- Location data or GPS coordinates
- Device identifiers or hardware information
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Crash reports or error logs transmitted to our servers
- Payment information (our applications do not process payments)
- Any data transmitted to Static Line LLC servers

---

## 2. How Your Data Is Stored

### 2.1 Local Storage

Our applications store data using browser local storage and IndexedDB on your device. This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to Static Line LLC or any third party.

### 2.2 Your Own Server Infrastructure

Some features of SCOUT allow data to be stored on a server you operate and control. Static Line LLC has no access to servers you operate. You are solely responsible for the security and management of data stored on your own infrastructure.

### 2.3 Synchronization Features

SCOUT's Sonar synchronization feature transfers data between devices on your local network or infrastructure. This data transfer occurs between machines you control and is not routed through Static Line LLC servers.

---

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your data with any third party. We have no access to the data you create or store within our applications.

---

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data is stored on your own device or server, you have full control over retention and deletion:

- **Browser data:** Clear your browser's site data or local storage to remove application data from your device.
- **Server data:** Delete data files from your server installation at any time.
- **Application uninstall:** Uninstalling the application may remove locally stored data depending on your operating system and browser.

## 6. California Residents — CCPA Rights

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) provides you with specific rights regarding your personal information. Because Static Line LLC does not collect, process, or store personal information from users of our applications, the following applies:

- **Right to Know:** We do not collect personal information about you.
- **Right to Delete:** Any data you have entered is stored on your own device or server and can be deleted by you at any time.
- **Right to Opt-Out of Sale:** We do not sell personal information.
- **Right to Non-Discrimination:** We do not discriminate against users who exercise their privacy rights.

To exercise any CCPA rights or for questions about this policy, contact us at: **support@static-line-llc.com**
