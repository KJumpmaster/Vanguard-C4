Built from operational experience

Vanguard C4 was built from experience, not theory.

Its foundation comes from 37 years of tactical and operational work, including 20 years of Army service and 17 years supporting tactical operations, combat missions, and advisory missions. That experience spans field operations and combat operations across Europe, the Middle East, and Central America.

It includes training soldiers at every level, from squad-level leadership through company, battalion, and brigade operations, including division-level staff work; developing and deploying operational tools; supporting joint and combined operations; and working in command environments where readiness, accountability, and mission clarity meant human life.

Vanguard C4 brings that mindset into a modern tactical operations platform: know your people, know your enemy, know your equipment, know your missions, and know when your team and squadron are ready to launch.

From field expedient to mission system

Norden II evolved from spreadsheet-based planning into a tactical loadout engine.

The early work began with the practical need to organize aircraft, stores, effects, mission purpose, and commander decision points in a form a team could actually use. Norden II is the current expression of that idea: a purpose-built loadout and mission-planning environment that replaces static tables with aircraft context, hardpoint visibility, package review, and command handoff.

Secure Cloud Sync

Approved data moves through Vanguard C4, not scattered file handoffs.

Vanguard C4 connects SkyPilot, Norden II, and S.C.O.U.T. through a secure cloud-sync layer backed by Azure. SkyPilot provides aircraft readiness and CPR data. Norden II provides validated loadouts, mission CPR, and strike-planning outputs. S.C.O.U.T. / SONAR brings those feeds together into the squadron command picture.

Using Azure as the backend, squadron data can be stored, updated, and distributed without manually passing files through Google Drive. Commanders, flight captains, sortie leads, and pilots can each receive the latest approved operational data for their role.

Command-control layer: S.C.O.U.T. is where the Decagon C2 operating logic becomes command rhythm: personnel, commander’s intent, skills, readiness, logistics / aircraft, intel assessment, mission ops, execution, AAR, and recognition.
