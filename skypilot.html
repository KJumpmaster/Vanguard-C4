SkyPilot

Know what is ready before you plan the mission.

SkyPilot is the aircraft registry and readiness source for Vanguard C4.

The module tracks aircraft inventory, readiness status, pilot certifications, ownership, notes, tactical banding, and combat-power indicators so commanders can understand the force before they assign the mission.

Registry and Readiness

The squadron’s aircraft picture starts here.

Before a commander can plan a strike package, the squadron needs to know which platforms exist, which are ready, who can fly them, and how that inventory translates into mission capacity.

What SkyPilot Owns

The readiness source.

SkyPilot should remain the authority for aircraft inventory and readiness data. Its job is to answer what aircraft the squadron has, what condition they are in, which pilots are qualified, and how much combat power is available.

01

Aircraft Registry

02

Pilot Status

Associate pilots with aircraft, qualification context, active status, and readiness signals that matter to command.

03

Certifications

Keep certifications and role qualifications visible so mission staffing can match the objective instead of guessing.

04

Combat Power

Translate aircraft and pilot readiness into a practical combat-power view that feeds mission and loadout decisions.

Readiness Flow

Readiness becomes useful when the rest of the suite can act on it.

SkyPilot is the beginning of the operational chain. Its outputs give Norden II better aircraft context and give S.C.O.U.T. a clearer picture of what the squadron can field.

01

Register Aircraft

02

03

Feed Planning

04

Update Command

Move approved changes into the command picture so readiness is current when the launch decision is made.

Connected to the Suite
